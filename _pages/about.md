## about me

I'm a Computer Scientist, and a graduate of Cambridge ([BA](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/computer-science-ba-hons-meng) 2024) and Oxford ([MSc](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-advanced-computer-science) 2025). At undergraduate, I achieved a **first with distinction** at undergraduate, ranking 5/113. I now work as a Software Engineer at [IMC](https://www.imc.com/eu).
